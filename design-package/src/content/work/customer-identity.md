## Bring the customer into focus

The same organization can appear differently across internal systems and outside data sources. Names vary, addresses change, and useful information ends up spread across many records.

I built a customer identity foundation that brings those pieces into one shared picture for sales, marketing, and fraud teams.

## Build a common point of reference

The golden record brings tens of millions of source records into 1.8 million distinct organizations. More than 150 stakeholders use that shared identity to work from a common understanding of the customer.

It also gives go-to-market AI workflows a consistent foundation for business context.

## Think beyond the first match

Identity is an ongoing responsibility. Matching, retention, and deletion all shape how a customer record stays useful over time. I approached them as parts of the same system.

## Make the picture actionable

Related work in industry classification, lead segmentation, and routing connects that customer picture to everyday decisions: which team should follow up, what an account needs, and where an opportunity belongs.

The value comes from helping different teams see the same customer clearly enough to act together.
