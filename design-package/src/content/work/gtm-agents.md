## Start with the decision someone needs to make

Sales and Marketing teams have plenty of information. The harder part is connecting it to a useful next step: understanding an account, recognizing intent, or deciding where to focus.

I built Sales and Marketing agents that bring those questions into a conversation, grounded in customer behavior, intent, and engagement data.

## Connect the foundation to the experience

The work spans the data underneath and the application on top. Curated datasets and a shared customer identity give focused agent workflows the business context they need. The interface turns that context into insight people can use in their day-to-day work.

That connection is where data engineering and applied AI come together for me.

## Learn from the people using it

I also built an evaluation and telemetry loop for a customer-facing AI assistant, using conversation patterns to inform agent design and product decisions. An internal requirements-validation tool explored the same idea earlier in the process: helping teams assess data and AI readiness before committing engineering effort.

## Carry the problem through delivery

The through-line is understanding the business question, building the right foundation, and staying involved as the system is used. Each stage helps shape the next.
