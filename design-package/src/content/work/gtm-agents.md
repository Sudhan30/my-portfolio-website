## Start with the work people are trying to do

Sales and Marketing teams need to understand customer behavior and decide where to focus. The useful question is often specific to an account, an interaction, or the next action a team could take.

My FDE résumé describes designing and deploying a Sales Agent and Marketing Agent for B2B go-to-market teams. The multi-agent workflows use LangGraph and draw on curated behavior, intent, and engagement datasets to support conversational insight and next-best-action recommendations.

## My contribution

The contribution spans data preparation and agent delivery. I worked on the datasets that give the agents business context and on the workflows that bring that context into a conversation with Sales, Marketing, and RevOps.

The foundation matters. My DE and FDE résumés describe ownership of a B2B platform with more than 50 production pipelines, including real-time and batch Salesforce integration. The platform surfaces more than $750M in order GMV for dashboards and reporting. That figure describes the business represented in the data, rather than revenue generated by the agents.

## The decision: make the business context useful first

The agents are grounded in curated datasets. Customer identity, behavior, and intent need to line up well enough for the response to mean something to the person making a decision.

That connects this work to the customer golden record: a shared identity layer that the FDE résumé also describes as a grounding layer for go-to-market agents. Data engineering and applied AI meet at that boundary.

## Close the loop with what happens in use

My FDE résumé also describes building the evaluation and telemetry loop for a customer-facing GenAI assistant. I instrumented web and mobile surfaces and analyzed conversation logs for B2B intent signals, turning usage patterns into agent design decisions and product strategy.

I also took an LLM-powered PRD validation tool from prototype to an adopted internal product, scoring requirements for data and AI readiness before engineering investment. These are related examples of moving from a model capability to a workflow people can use.

## What I want the story to communicate

The through-line is ownership across discovery, the data foundation, application delivery, and the feedback loop. The model is one part of that system.

This account does not attach an accuracy score or a revenue result to the agents. Those outcomes would need a defined measurement method and evidence beyond the résumé.
