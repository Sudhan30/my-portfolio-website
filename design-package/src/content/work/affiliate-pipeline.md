## A channel needs a data path

An acquisition channel becomes easier to understand when its activity has a dependable path into the business’s data systems. The engineering has to connect the source to information that people can use.

My DE and FDE résumés describe independently designing, building, and deploying the affiliate marketing data pipeline end to end for the B2B business.

## My contribution

The résumé reports 4.9 million new customer visits and $5.4 million in GMV in the first production year, with the channel later scaling to an annual run rate above $50 million.

The important part of the contribution is end-to-end ownership: a new channel needed an implemented data pipeline, and the work was carried through deployment. This story does not infer a specific technical stack or architecture for that pipeline beyond what the résumés state.

## The decision: keep the business question close

The outcome is described in customer acquisition and GMV terms because those connect the pipeline to the channel’s purpose. A successfully processed record is useful evidence of execution; understanding how a channel contributes is the business question above it.

These figures are résumé-reported channel outcomes. GMV describes merchandise value, not net revenue or a measured causal effect attributable solely to the pipeline.

## Related work: agree on what credit means

In a separate contribution, I partnered with sales leadership on last-touch attribution across the B2B customer base. The FDE résumé frames this as technical discovery: defining how conversion credit should work, then designing and shipping the methodology.

Both résumés describe capture moving from about 35% toward a 95% target. The 95% figure is a target, not a confirmed attained outcome, so this site does not present it as a result.

## Delivery continues after launch

My broader platform ownership includes data quality and freshness SLOs, automated validation and monitoring, and P0/P1 incident command. That operating experience informs how I think about pipelines after their first successful run.
