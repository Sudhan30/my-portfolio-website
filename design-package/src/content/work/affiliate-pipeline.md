## Give a new channel a dependable data path

A new acquisition channel creates a practical engineering question: how does its activity become information the business can understand and use?

I designed, built, and deployed the affiliate marketing data pipeline end to end, connecting channel activity to the B2B data platform.

## Keep the purpose in view

In its first production year, the channel reached 4.9 million new customer visits and $5.4 million in GMV. Those outcomes give the pipeline a clear business context: understanding acquisition and the merchandise value associated with the channel.

## Agree on what the signals mean

In related work, I partnered with sales leadership to define and deliver last-touch attribution across the B2B customer base. That work began with a shared understanding of conversion credit before becoming a technical implementation.

A useful pipeline carries both the data and a clear meaning for the people reading it.

## Stay responsible after launch

My broader platform work includes data quality, freshness, monitoring, and incident response. That experience shapes how I think about delivery: a system needs to keep making sense long after its first successful run.
