## A new channel, no data path

A new acquisition channel brings a practical question. How does a partner’s click become something the business can read on Monday morning?

I designed, built, and deployed the affiliate marketing pipeline end to end, from partner activity to the B2B data platform.

## What it carried

In its first production year, the channel brought 4.9 million new customer visits and $5.4 million in GMV. Those outcomes are why the pipeline mattered: they let the business judge a channel it had just started paying for.

## The decision: agree on credit first

A customer often touches several channels before ordering. Who gets the credit sounds technical, but it is a business decision. I partnered with sales leadership to define last-touch attribution across the B2B customer base before building it, so the number on the dashboard meant the same thing to everyone reading it.

## Late is not low

Partner data arrives on its own schedule. A file that lands late can make a healthy day look like a bad one. My platform work covers data quality, freshness, monitoring, and incident response, and it shapes one habit here: a late file should be flagged as late, not published as a dip.
